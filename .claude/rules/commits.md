# Commits

Use Conventional Commits (`<type>[optional scope]: <description>`) with the
types `feat`, `fix`, `refactor`, `test`, `docs`, `build`, `ci` and `ai`. Mark a
breaking change with a `BREAKING CHANGE:` footer.

Do not commit on your own. Present the changes with a proposed commit message
and wait for explicit approval. Approving a step of the work is not an approval
to commit.
